# Brag Plan: PureCanvas

## What is this app?
A local-first vector design app that runs entirely in the browser tab: draw with
text, images, lines, shapes, icons and a pen on an infinite dot-grid canvas, then
export to SVG, PNG or WEBP. No account, no server, no uploads.

## The angle
Every design tool wants your email, your cloud, your subscription. PureCanvas
opens on a dot grid and just lets you draw. The film plays as a quiet, confident
tool demo: the canvas gets used in front of you, the UI assembles around the
thing you made, and the punchline is everything the product *doesn't* ask for.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black dot grid. A cursor drags a periwinkle rectangle into existence in half a
second. The words **"design, in a tab."** slam in over it and hold.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The real editor assembles around the shape: top bar, tool rail, properties panel.
- Text gets typed straight onto the canvas, then a star is drawn and both are
  selected — eight grips, rotation handle, live property sliders.
- Click Export → the menu drops → `poster.svg`, `poster.png`, `poster.webp` fly out.
- Three crossed-out asks land one by one: No account / No server / No uploads.

## Outro / punchline
Logo, **PureCanvas**, "free design.", **purecanvas.app**. Music ends on a bar.

## User flow worth showing
Entry: the empty infinite canvas is already there, no sign-up wall.
Key action: draw a rectangle → type text → draw a star → select and transform.
Result: click Export, three real file formats appear.

## Tone
- Preset: `default`
- Creative direction: "quiet, confident anti-signup tool film — the design app
  that behaves like a text editor"
- Interpretation: playful but restrained. Fast, physical motion on the canvas and
  the UI; no hype copy, no fake metrics, no mockup gloss. The humor is that a real
  design app needs nothing from you.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 21.0 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#0a0a0a` (page theme-color), panels `#121212`
- Accent: `#8b8bf7` (default shape fill in the editor)
- Text: `#ededed` (foreground), muted `#8a8a8a`
- Hairlines: `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`
- Display font: Geist Variable (the app's own font, bundled from the project)
- Body font: Geist Variable
- Strongest visual element: the infinite dot grid + the floating right-hand tool
  rail, plus the periwinkle element fill against near-black.

## Share copy (draft)
Design app in a browser tab. No account, no server, no uploads — draw it, export
the SVG, close the tab.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, lightly electronic bed that carries pacing; sparse motion-matched accents.
- Music: bundled `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (120.19 BPM).
- Music treatment: start at 0 with a short fade-in, sit under the SFX, fade under
  the final logo and end on a bar line.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset
  `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`.
  Strong cues to target: 13.01s (export payoff) and 18.02s (logo landing).
  Beat grid for sequential events: 11.02, 11.52, 12.02 for the three export files.
  Restraint: no cue on every cut; the UI reveals run on their own motion.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — the canvas dot grid brightens slightly with
  the music's low-band energy, never a waveform or equalizer.
- SFX posture: sparse to moderate, motion-matched, dry.
- Audio-coupled moments: shape drag (soft pencil/drag), text typed (key ticks),
  star draw, selection click, menu open (click), file chips (three pops), final
  logo (one impact).
- Restraint rule: no whoosh on every cut, no chimes, nothing over the last line.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook: the shape and the line — 3.0s
Dot grid fades up from black. A cursor drags a 560x300 rounded rectangle with the
app's periwinkle fill across the frame. At 1.15s **"design, in a tab."** lands in
Geist 900, dead still, over the canvas.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated cursor drag draws the rectangle.
Audio intent: a soft, physical drag; the first musical beat (3.02s) is the seam.
Audio-coupled idea: one drag texture; nothing on the type.
Music: bed enters quietly at 0, kick lands at 3.02.
Transition mood: hard cut into the editor → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The app assembles — 3.6s
The same rectangle stays. The real editor chrome builds around it: top bar slides
down from the top edge, the right tool rail (Select, Text, Image, Line, Shape,
Icon, Pen) slides in, the properties panel slides in from the left. Headline
"A full vector design app." with sub "in your browser." settles over the canvas.
Sequential/interaction: yes — chrome pieces arrive one after another (0.12s stagger).
Audio intent: satisfying, mechanical, quiet — pieces clicking into place.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft woodblock/click per chrome piece.
Music: bed continues.
Transition mood: soft — headline clears → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Make something — 3.8s
Cursor picks the Text tool (rail highlights), clicks the canvas and types
"Design anything." character by character. Then it draws a star next to the text.
Select-all: both get selection boxes with eight grips and a rotation handle, and
the properties panel sliders animate. Caption bottom-left: "Text. Images. Shapes.
Icons. Pen."
Sequential/interaction: yes — tool select, type, draw star, select.
Audio intent: tactile, fast; typing ticks carry the middle.
Audio-coupled idea: key ticks per word, one pencil stroke for the star, one click
for selection.
Music: bed continues, slight lift.
Transition mood: hard cut with a small punch-in → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Export — 4.0s
Cursor clicks the top-right Export button; a dark menu drops with SVG, PNG, WEBP
and Copy PNG. Three file chips fly out one by one on the beat: `poster.svg`,
`poster.png`, `poster.webp`. Headline: "Export SVG, PNG, WEBP." Sub: "Fonts and
images embedded."
Sequential/interaction: yes — click, menu, three chips on consecutive beats.
Audio intent: crisp and finished; the three chips are the percussion.
Audio-coupled idea: menu click, then three pops on 11.02 / 11.52 / 12.02; the
payoff lands near the strong cue at 13.01.
Music: bed peaks here.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — What it doesn't ask for — 3.2s
The canvas dims back to bare dot grid. Three short lines land one at a time,
each with a thin strike-through rule drawing across the word before it:
"No account." / "No server." / "No uploads." Then a quiet sub-line: "Everything
saves in your browser."
Sequential/interaction: yes — three lines, readable spacing, then the sub-line.
Audio intent: dead air, then dry thuds; the joke is the silence.
Audio-coupled idea: three low, dry impacts on the line landings.
Music: bed drops back, low-pass feel.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Outro — 3.4s
Logo mark and **PureCanvas** land dead centre on the strong cue at 18.02s,
with "free design." under it and **purecanvas.app** at the bottom. Music fades.
Sequential/interaction: none beyond the logo landing.
Audio intent: one impact, then clean air.
Audio-coupled idea: single logo hit; music fades under it.
Music: fade out over 0.8s.
Transition mood: end.

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat, warm, minimal-electronic, 120 BPM.
**Audio summary:** a friendly 120 BPM bed enters under the hook, needle-drops into
the editor with the first kick, builds through make and export, drops out for the
three-line punchline, and lands once on the logo.
